Our Story

Founded by
Educators, Built for

Etrain was founded in 2019 by a team of educators and technologists who saw a gap between traditional education and the skills employers need.

What started as a small collection of web development courses has grown into a comprehensive platform serving learners across 45 countries.

  • Over 250 courses across 12 categories
  • Industry partnerships with leading tech companies
